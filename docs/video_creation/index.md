# Video creation

Currently, the only platform I upload videos to, is YouTube. My general philosophy towards video creation, is to never upload something I wouldn't watch myself, as well as to try not to hop on trending topics, unless they actually interest me.

I choose to only make Beat Saber videos for the time being. Initially I picked Beat Saber because I enjoyed the game and couldn't really find a content creator that I enjoyed watching. I haven't regretted this so far, and I still believe there is a lot of potential to create entertaining Beat Saber content, despite the lack of interesting updates the game receives. What makes this game special to me, is the community and what it has been able to achieve without much in the way of support from the devs.
